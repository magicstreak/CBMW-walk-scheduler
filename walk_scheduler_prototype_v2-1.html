Walk Programme Scheduler — Prototype

Constraint-based scheduling test: October to May • Wednesday & Saturday • E / M / MS / S / VS

What this prototype does

It creates every Wednesday and Saturday in the selected October–May season, with one slot for each grade. It then chooses eligible leaders using availability, while enforcing one walk per grade per day and one walk per leader per day. The scoring favours under-filled grades/months and leaders with fewer assignments.
